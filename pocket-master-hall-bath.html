Master & Hall Bath Remodel

A full remodel of both the master and hall bathrooms. The master bath was taken down to the studs and reconfigured for a curbless walk-in shower with heated floors, dual shower heads (including a rain head), and a freestanding spa tub with heated controls — plus a redesigned closet layout for maximum storage. The hall bath got a full tub and surround replacement, new vanity, pocket door upgrade, and removal of an old exterior staircase and door. The Pocket, Sacramento, CA.
