About Bevel Builders

A contractor you can actually trust.

Family-rooted, locally owned, and built on the belief that construction clients deserve honesty, craftsmanship, and a partner who shows up.

Bevel Builders, Inc. was founded on a simple idea: construction clients deserve honesty, craftsmanship, and a partner who shows up. No surprise invoices. No subpar finishes. No excuses. We're licensed, bonded, and fully insured — and every member of our team is vetted for skill and character. Based in El Dorado Hills, California, we proudly serve homeowners and businesses throughout Sacramento County, El Dorado County, and Placer County. CA General Contractor License #1113839.

I grew up on a small family ranch, where "land rich, cash poor" wasn't just a saying — it was a way of life. If something was broken, you fixed it, and if you needed something, you built it. My grandfather, a mechanical engineer who'd once held his own contractor's license and built several homes locally, was the center of that world. We built rubber band race cars and kid-sized workbenches together, and when I was 10, I had the privilege of helping him build the large shop on our property — the same shop where my younger brother and I spent years tinkering with woodworking.

Without cable growing up, my TV options were limited to East Coast satellite feeds — so my brother and I grew up watching Bob Vila and This Old House instead of typical kids' programming. Combined with a mechanical mind and years in the high school woodshop, construction wasn't just an interest — it was in my blood.

College was a non-negotiable in my family, a value forged by the struggles of running a small farm, ranch, and orchard. I started in accounting, where math came naturally, but it wasn't until I discovered Construction Management at Chico State that things clicked.

That set me on a 15-year path in commercial construction, working my way up from project engineer to superintendent to preconstruction manager on large institutional and corporate projects across the Bay Area and Sacramento — schools, hospitals, fire stations, lab and clean room facilities, and corporate campuses. That experience made me genuinely comfortable with larger, more complex projects and gave me deep familiarity working with various municipalities — navigating permitting, inspections, and approvals that often intimidate smaller contractors.

But through it all, my roots stayed residential. After 15 years on the commercial side, I got my own license to come back to what started this for me — helping families create a home. It's a bit of a cliché path — grandson learns from grandfather, grows up, comes back to his roots — but cliché or not, it's my story, and I wouldn't trade it.

Today, that's what Bevel Builders is built on. I run this business alongside my continued work in commercial construction management — a balance that lets me stay selective, take on a limited number of projects at a time, and give each one the attention it deserves. Whether you're planning a custom home in Granite Bay, a commercial build-out in Roseville, a kitchen remodel in Folsom, or a room addition in Cameron Park, my goal isn't just to build a space — it's to guide you through the process with the experience and care that makes it a simply positive experience, start to finish.
