Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Bevel Builders, Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website located at bevelbuilders.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our site or submit a contact form.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information you voluntarily provide through our contact form, which may include:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Project type and description

We do not use analytics tracking, advertising pixels, or third-party tracking cookies on this website.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through our contact form is used solely to respond to your inquiry and follow up regarding your construction project. We do not use your information for marketing, and we do not add you to any mailing lists without your explicit consent.

3. How We Store Your Information

Contact form submissions are processed through Formspree, a third-party form service. Formspree may temporarily store submission data on their servers. You can review Formspree's privacy policy at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

We do not maintain our own database of contact form submissions beyond what is delivered to our email inbox.

4. We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to any third parties for commercial purposes.

5. Cookies

Our website uses a single functional cookie to remember that you have acknowledged our cookie notice. This cookie contains no personal information and is not used for tracking or advertising purposes.

6. California Residents — Your CCPA Rights

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Know: You may request information about what personal data we have collected about you.
  • Right to Delete: You may request that we delete any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Opt Out: We do not sell personal data, so there is nothing to opt out of.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at (916) 572-4818.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
