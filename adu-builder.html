Services · ADUs

ADUs (accessory dwelling units) in El Dorado Hills and the Sacramento area

Whether it's for family, guests or rental income, we take an ADU from "can I build one here?" through permits, construction and final inspection.

What's included

  • Site visit and feasibility: setbacks, utilities and access
  • Design coordination, plans and engineering
  • Permit submittal and inspections with your city or county
  • Foundation, framing, roofing and utilities
  • Kitchen, bathroom and interior finishes
  • Garage conversions and attached ADUs

How it works

  1. Consultation. We meet at your home, walk the space and talk through goals, must-haves and budget.
  2. Design and scope. We coordinate layout, selections and drawings, or work with your architect or designer.
  3. Permits. We prepare and submit the permit package and schedule the required inspections.
  4. Build. Vetted trade partners do the work; Amon oversees the schedule, quality and communication.
  5. Final walkthrough. We walk the finished space with you and close out the punch list.

Common questions

Can I build an ADU on my lot?

Many single-family lots in California can have one, but setbacks, utilities and local rules vary. We start with a site visit and check with your city or county.

Detached, attached or garage conversion: which is best?

It depends on your lot, budget and how you'll use the space. A garage conversion is usually the fastest; a detached unit gives the most privacy.

Who handles the permits?

We do. We prepare and submit the package and schedule inspections.

Can I rent out an ADU?

Rules depend on your city or county. We'll point you to the current local requirements before you commit.
